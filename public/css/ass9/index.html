FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does it cost to hire on Upwork?

Posting a job is free. Upwork charges a small marketplace fee once you and a professional agree to work together, and you only pay when work is delivered.

How do I find the right freelancer?

Post a job and review proposals, browse the Project Catalog for fixed-price services, or work with a Talent Agency for full teams. Uma helps you shortlist faster.

Are payments protected?

Yes. Funds are held in escrow for fixed-price contracts and released by milestone. Hourly contracts are covered by payment protection when using time tracking.

Can I hire talent internationally?

Absolutely. Our marketplace spans 180+ countries and 10,000+ skills, with compliance, contracts, and invoicing handled in one place.
